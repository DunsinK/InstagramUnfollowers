Instagram Unfollowers

Ever wonder who’s unfollowed you on Instagram?

This tool uses Instagram's official Download Your Data feature to safely check who isn't following you back — no scraping, no bots, and everything runs locally on your device.

How to Download Your Instagram Data

Follow these steps or watch the tutorial below:

  • Go to Settings → Your Activity / Privacy.
  • Select Download Your Information.
  • Request your data (followers + following only for fastest results).
  • Extract the ZIP and locate following.html and followers_1.html.
  • Upload both files below.
